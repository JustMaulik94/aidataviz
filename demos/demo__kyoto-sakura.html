Kyoto Cherry Blossom Dates, 812–2015 CE

Day-of-year for peak bloom. Data: Aono & Kazui (2008), extended to 2015.

Before — Connect-the-Dots

A noisy line connecting 1,200 years of annual records. Seasonal patterns and long-run climate signals are both present but difficult to separate from year-to-year variation. Legend, colored line, and full border follow conventional practice.

After — Dot Scatter + Running Median + Climate Epoch Bands

Kyoto cherry blossom peak bloom date, 812–2015 CE. Each dot is one year's observation. The solid line is a 30-year running median, separating climate signal from inter-annual noise. Epoch bands show the Medieval Warm Period (warm, earlier blooms), the Little Ice Age (cool, later blooms), and the post-industrial era. The dashed reference is the pre-industrial median (c.1500–1850). Red circle marks the record-early bloom of 2021 (March 26, day 85).

What the skill changed

  • Dots + running median instead of connected line. Raw observations plot as semi-transparent gray dots (the noise). A 30-year running median overlays in black (the signal). The reader sees both without either obscuring the other.
  • Climate epoch bands as contextual underlayer. Tinted rectangles for the Medieval Warm Period (amber), Little Ice Age (blue), and Industrial era (red) sit behind the data, providing interpretive scaffolding without fighting with the dots.
  • Range frame instead of chart box. The four-sided border is replaced by two axis lines that span only the data range. This encodes the data extent rather than an arbitrary frame.
  • Axis inversion made explicit. Earlier bloom dates are higher on the y-axis; the label reads "Bloom date (earlier ↑)" so the direction of the axis is immediately clear.
  • Pre-industrial median as single reference line. One dashed horizontal line encodes the long-run baseline without adding grid noise.
  • Direct annotation of the record value. A red circle and year label call out the 2021 record without requiring a separate legend or caption reference.
  • No legend. The context is encoded in the epoch bands and axis label. There is no series legend to consult.
  • Background chartjunk removed. No gridlines, no border box, off-white background (#fdfdfb).
